4. Staged transfer roadmap

The planner prioritises a five-week points outcome while respecting free-transfer rollover, squad composition, the three-player club limit and available budget. It avoids hits unless the expected gain exceeds the hit cost. Selling prices are approximated from current market prices unless manually adjusted in the squad editor.

5. Important assumptions and controls

  • FPL API data is refreshed on demand. This page does not store historical snapshots.
  • Expected points are estimates derived from current API statistics and fixture difficulty; they are not bookmaker odds or official predictions.
  • Current screenshot does not show bank balance, free transfers, or individual selling prices. Defaults are £0.0m and one free transfer; edit these above.
  • FPL selling prices can differ from current market prices because of purchase-price profit rules. The squad editor includes an optional selling-price override per player.
  • Chip use and special rules can change the optimal strategy. This version treats your remaining Free Hit as unused but does not assume using it.
