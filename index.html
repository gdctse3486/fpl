How learning worksOnly predictions captured before a deadline are scored. Completed-gameweek actuals are retrieved from FPL live data. Recent, observed player-level forecast errors gently calibrate future projections; the adjustment is capped, weighted towards recent gameweeks and only activates after a minimum sample. History is stored in this browser and can be exported/imported to share or back it up.
Unofficial FPL companion. Projections are estimates, not guaranteed scores. Player availability, minutes, line-ups, postponements and FPL API changes can materially affect results.
